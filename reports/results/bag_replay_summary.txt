llm-nav-shield: replay on local costmaps built from a public ROS bag
====================================================================

Data (not committed; tools/bag_to_scenarios.py derives everything from it):
  b2-2016-04-05-14-44-52.bag, Google's public Cartographer backpack_2d set
  https://storage.googleapis.com/cartographer-public-data/bags/backpack_2d/
  sha256 095bd3b04799a5f69158faa6c6fe3ef4f1e4b7a0b02e91e85344b0d4787f1bcb
  89.6 MB, 360 s, horizontal_laser_2d: 1079 beams, 269.5 deg, 13205 scans

Forty scans sampled evenly become forty robot-centred 10 m x 10 m local
costmaps at 0.05 m. Rays clear the cells they cross and mark their return
lethal; cells no ray reaches stay unknown. The robot footprint is cleared,
as nav2's costmap does.

How real maps differ from the committed synthetic forty
-------------------------------------------------------
                            committed 40        bag 40
  unknown cells             0.88%               33.4% to 90.1%, median 55.0%
  free cells on the edge    0 of 800            26 to 444, median 216
  world origin              always (0, 0)       robot-centred

Proposals
---------
qwen2.5:7b-instruct at temperature 0, through the upstream llm_eval
collect.py and parse.py unmodified, same prompt template. 40/40 responses
parsed to waypoint lists. Median 10.4 s per response on CPU.

Result, interior goals (a cell the planner would accept)
--------------------------------------------------------
  forwarded_safe          1
  recovered_from_unsafe   37
  recovered_from_off_goal 1
  halted_no_safe_path     1
  fallback_unsafe         0
  sealed-goal halt suite  10/10, 0 violations
  decision time           median 2.06 ms, max 4.23 ms

qwen produced a plan that was both safe and goal-reaching in 1 of 40 real
scenarios, against 2 of 40 on the synthetic set. The model is about as
unreliable on real costmaps as on procedural ones.

Frame invariance
----------------
Every scenario was replayed twice, once with the window corner at the
world origin and once at the robot-centred origin (-5, -5), with start,
goal and every waypoint moved by the same offset.

  bag suite, 40 scenarios + 10 sealed variants : 0 bucket mismatches
  committed suite at (-5,-5), (-12.2,-8.4), (100,250) : 0 mismatches

One known instability, unchanged by any of this work: at origin
(3.75, 101.5) committed scenario 12 moves from recovered_from_off_goal to
recovered_from_unsafe, because its waypoints sit exactly on cell
boundaries and the world-to-map division lands one on the other side.
Both outcomes are recoveries, so nothing unsafe is forwarded.

What the boundary fix was worth, measured
-----------------------------------------
The pre-fix shield was rebuilt from cb88d20 and run on the same grids.

  goals      proposals      recoveries   pre-fix        fixed
                                         fallback_unsafe
  interior   straight line  13           0              0
  interior   qwen           39           0              0
  edge       straight line  40 attempts  14             0

Free cells on the map edge are not enough on their own to trigger the
bug: with interior goals the pre-fix and fixed shields agree on every one
of 40 scenarios, even with 39 recovery paths computed. The bug fires on
goal placement. With the goal on the window boundary, where nav2 puts a
local goal because that is where the global plan leaves the window, the
pre-fix shield produced 14 unsafe fallbacks out of 40 and the fixed
shield produced none, halting on all 40 instead.

That halting is correct but blunt: a goal three cells from the window
edge is not a pose the robot can occupy without its footprint leaving the
known map. A deployment would need to project the local goal inward or
pad the window. The shield as it stands refuses.

What this does not show
-----------------------
A backpack lidar walked through a museum, not a wheeled robot under
closed-loop control. The bag carries no odometry and no TF, so these are
single-scan local windows, not accumulated maps, and nothing here tests
transform timing or drift. Goal selection is a heuristic in
tools/bag_to_scenarios.py, and the two policies give different answers,
which is itself the main finding.

Reproduce
---------
  pip install rosbags numpy
  curl -LO https://storage.googleapis.com/cartographer-public-data/bags/backpack_2d/b2-2016-04-05-14-44-52.bag
  python3 tools/bag_to_scenarios.py --bag b2-2016-04-05-14-44-52.bag --out /tmp/scen --count 40
  # proposals, or reuse the committed reports/bag/parsed/*.txt
  python3 tools/bag_replay.py --scenarios /tmp/scen \
      --parsed reports/bag/parsed/qwen2.5-7b-instruct.txt --out /tmp/replay
